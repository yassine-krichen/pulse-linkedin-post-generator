## Quick Examples

### Example: Quick Insight

```markdown
---
Title: Why Full-Stack Visibility Matters in Production
Type: Standard Post
Author: Krichenyassine
Date: 2025-04-15
Status: Ready for Review
Word Count: 185
---

## Post Copy

### Hook

I spent a week debugging a memory leak that only showed up under load. It wasn't in the code — it was in how the frontend triggered the backend.

### Body

This is the advantage of being full-stack: you see the whole conversation. The frontend was making requests faster than the backend could process them. The frontend logs looked fine. The backend logs looked fine. But the system as a whole was broken.

Most engineers see their half of the system. They optimize what they can control and assume the other side is working. Full-stack engineers see the seams.

### Takeaway

System problems rarely live in one layer. They live in the contract between layers.

### CTA

What's a system-wide bug you only saw after zooming out?
```

## Pro Tips

- **Write the hook first** and make sure it's specific (not "I learned something")
- **Use specific numbers, names, or technical terms** — makes it feel real
- **One clear principle per post** — don't overload
- **Read it aloud** — check for rhythm and naturalness
- **Remove clichés** — no "game-changer," "no days off," "passion," etc.
- **Visual should elevate, not repeat** — choose image only if it adds new information or mood
