# Prompt: AI Image Generation Prompt Creator

Use this prompt to convert a concept or visual idea into a detailed, Gemini-compatible image generation prompt.

---

## Input

**User provides:**
- Concept or visual idea (usually tied to a post or carousel slide)
- (Optional) Specific mood, focus area, or style preferences

---

## Your Task

Generate a detailed, natural-language prompt that produces a clean, minimal, professional image aligned with the brand aesthetic. The user will paste this directly into Gemini and download the result.

---

## Brand Aesthetic Requirements (Non-Negotiable)

Every image prompt must include references to:

- **Color palette:** Black, white, off-white, dark grays — no colors unless a single precise accent
- **Style:** Editorial tech photography, high-end minimal design, prestigious
- **Vibe:** Professional, expensive, minimal — not cheerful or startup-y
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif (if visible) — sharp, clean, editorial
- **Subject:** Abstract, technical, conceptual, or editorial — not stock photo smiles
- **Tone:** Confident, sophisticated, thoughtful

---

## Prompt Structure

A strong image prompt for Gemini should include:

1. **Main subject/concept** — What is the image about?
2. **Composition** — How are elements arranged?
3. **Style/aesthetic** — Clean, minimal, editorial, etc.
4. **Color palette** — Black, white, grays, (optional single accent)
5. **Mood/atmosphere** — Sophisticated, professional, technical
6. **Details to avoid** — No clichés, no cheerfulness, no busy elements
7. **Technical note** — High quality, professional, premium feel

---

## Generation Process

1. **Understand the concept**
   - What is the core visual idea?
   - What feeling or message should it convey?

2. **Choose the visual approach**
   - Abstract/geometric (clean lines, shapes)?
   - Technical illustration (diagrams, code, hardware)?
   - Editorial photograph (styled scene)?
   - Mixed/hybrid (combination)?

3. **Build the color hierarchy**
   - Primary: Black or dark gray background?
   - Secondary: White or light text/elements?
   - Accent: Single color if appropriate? (keep muted)

4. **Describe composition**
   - Left/right alignment, center focus, layered, etc.
   - What's prominent, what's subtle?

5. **Add atmospheric detail**
   - Lighting: Clean, stark, minimal?
   - Texture: Smooth, sharp, glossy, or matte?
   - Depth: Flat, layered, dimensional?

6. **Reference brand aesthetic**
   - Why does this feel "prestigious"?
   - What makes it feel high-end vs. generic?

7. **Specify what to avoid**
   - No colorful gradients
   - No cheesy fonts or emojis
   - No stock photo people
   - No motivational vibes
   - No clutter

8. **Close with quality descriptor**
   - "Professional 8K render," "Editorial photography," "High-quality design," etc.


## Tips for Best Results in Gemini

1. Paste the entire prompt as-is into Gemini's image generation field
2. If the result feels too colorful, regenerate and add: "Reduce all colors to black, white, and gray with no other hues"
3. If it feels too cheerful, regenerate and add: "Make it more sophisticated and editorial, less startup-y"
4. If it feels generic, regenerate and add: "Make it premium and high-end, like luxury tech design magazine photography"
5. Save successful prompts — you can reuse the structure for similar concepts

## Key Principles

- **Be specific but leave room for AI interpretation** — don't over-constrain
- **Reference the aesthetic, not just the subject** — "like luxury tech magazine" or "editorial and sophisticated" helps shape the feel
- **Avoid common clichés** — "no stock photos," "no smiling people," "no motivational posters"
- **Use "muted," "dark," "subtle" to keep colors minimal** — never say "bright" or "colorful"
- **Always close with a quality descriptor** — "8K," "editorial," "professional," "premium"
- **Test and iterate** — if Gemini's result doesn't match, regenerate with more specific constraints
